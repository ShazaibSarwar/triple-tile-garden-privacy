Triple Tile Garden – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026

This policy explains how the Android mobile game Triple Tile Garden (package com.shazaib.tripletilegarden), published by Shazaib Sarwar ("we", "us"), handles information. Triple Tile Garden is a single-player puzzle game. It has no accounts, sign-in, chat or developer-operated server. The game displays ads.

Information stored by the game

We do not directly collect, store or receive personal information. The game does not use an analytics or developer-operated crash-reporting service.

Game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. This includes unlocked levels, stars, coins, boosters, daily reward history, tutorial status, sound and music settings, vibration settings and your selected tile style. This information is not uploaded to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Triple Tile Garden uses Google AdMob to show banner ads on the home and gameplay screens, interstitial ads between some completed levels, and optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch for in-game rewards or assistance.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share the following information for advertising, analytics, ad measurement, security and fraud-prevention purposes:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location
  • Device and account identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID and app set ID
  • User interactions, such as app launches, taps, ad impressions, clicks and video views
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as app launch time, crashes, hangs and energy use
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language and app version

Google handles this information under its own policies. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Consent and your advertising choices

  • Consent messages: the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform. Where required, a consent or privacy message is shown before ads are requested. Your selection may affect whether ads are personalised.
  • Change your choice: when required for your region, open the game's Settings screen and select Ad Privacy Options to review or change your privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: Android provides controls to reset or delete your Advertising ID, usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Rewarded ads: rewarded videos are optional. Choosing not to watch one does not prevent you from playing the game.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. We do not share local game progress or settings with other companies. Google and its advertising partners may process the advertising data described above according to their policies and your consent choices.

Data retention and deletion

Local progress and settings remain on your device until you remove them. You can use Settings → Restart Progress to delete saved game progress. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored game data. Google retains advertising data under its own retention policies. Requests concerning information processed by Google should be made through Google's privacy controls.

Permissions and offline play

The app uses internet and network-state access to load ads and uses vibration for optional haptic feedback. It does not request access to your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or files. Core gameplay works without an internet connection, but ads and ad-based rewards are not available while offline.

Children's privacy

Triple Tile Garden is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an advertising service, contact us so we can assist with the appropriate deletion request.

Security

Local game data is protected by the Android app sandbox. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we limit the information handled by the game and do not operate a server that receives player data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the game, its services or legal requirements change. The current version will remain available on this page, with the effective date updated above.
